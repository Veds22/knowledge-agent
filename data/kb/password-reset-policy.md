# Password Reset Policy

## Overview
All employee passwords must comply with the company's security policy. Passwords expire every 90 days.

## Password Requirements
- Minimum 12 characters.
- Must include: uppercase letter, lowercase letter, number, and special character (!@#$%^&*).
- Cannot reuse the last 10 passwords.
- Cannot contain your name, username, or company name.

## How to Reset Your Password

### Self-Service Reset (Recommended)
1. Go to `https://passwordreset.internal`.
2. Enter your corporate email address.
3. Choose verification method: Microsoft Authenticator app or backup email.
4. Follow the verification steps.
5. Enter and confirm your new password.
6. Sign out of all active sessions and sign back in with the new password.

### Reset via IT Helpdesk
If self-service fails:
1. Call IT Helpdesk at ext. 1001 (available 8am–6pm Mon–Fri).
2. Provide your employee ID for identity verification.
3. IT will send a temporary password to your manager's email.
4. Log in with the temporary password and change it immediately.

## Account Lockout Policy
- Account locks after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
- Lockout duration: 30 minutes (auto-unlock) or immediate unlock via IT.
- After 3 lockouts in one day, manual IT verification is required.

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- MFA is mandatory for all employees.
- Approved MFA methods: Microsoft Authenticator app (preferred), SMS to registered mobile.
- To register or change MFA device: visit `https://mfa.internal` or contact IT.
- Lost MFA device: call ext. 1001 immediately — do not wait.
