# Hardware Request Process

## Overview
All hardware procurement goes through IT to ensure compatibility, licensing, and asset tracking.

## Standard Hardware Catalogue
| Item | Spec | Approval Required |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop (Windows) | Dell Latitude / Lenovo ThinkPad | Manager |
| Laptop (macOS) | MacBook Pro 14" M3 | Manager |
| Monitor | 27" 4K Dell UltraSharp | Manager |
| Keyboard/Mouse | Logitech MX series | Self-service |
| Headset | Jabra Evolve2 55 | Self-service |
| Docking Station | Dell WD22TB4 | Manager |
| External SSD | Samsung T7 1TB | Manager |

## How to Submit a Request
1. Log in to `https://itportal.internal/hardware`.
2. Select item from the catalogue or enter a custom request.
3. Provide business justification and select your cost centre.
4. Submit for manager approval (auto-routed by the portal).
5. Once approved, IT Procurement processes within 3–5 business days.
6. Delivery to office: 5–7 business days. Remote delivery: 7–10 business days.

## Replacement Hardware
- Devices older than 4 years are eligible for standard refresh.
- Damaged hardware: raise a P3 ticket with photos — IT assesses repair vs replace.
- Lost or stolen hardware: raise a P1 ticket immediately for remote wipe.

## Setup and Onboarding
- New devices are pre-configured by IT before delivery.
- Data migration from old to new device: request via the hardware ticket.
- Old devices must be returned to IT within 5 business days of receiving the replacement.

## Personal Device (BYOD) Policy
- BYOD is permitted only with prior IT approval and MDM enrolment.
- Submit a BYOD request at `https://itportal.internal/byod`.
- Company data on personal devices is subject to remote wipe if the device is reported lost.
