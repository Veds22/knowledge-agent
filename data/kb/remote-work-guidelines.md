# Remote Work Guidelines

## Overview
Remote work is available to eligible employees. All remote workers must comply with this policy to maintain security and productivity standards.

## Eligibility
- Full-time employees after completing the 90-day probation period.
- Role must be approved for remote work by the employee's manager and HR.
- Part-time remote and full-remote arrangements require separate HR approval.

## Technical Requirements

### Mandatory
- Company-issued device (BYOD requires prior MDM enrolment — see Hardware Policy).
- Cisco AnyConnect VPN connected for all work on internal systems.
- Stable internet connection: minimum 20 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload.
- Microsoft Teams or Slack available and responsive during core hours.

### Recommended
- Wired ethernet connection (more stable than WiFi for video calls).
- UPS or surge protector for equipment.
- External monitor and ergonomic setup.

## Security Requirements
- Never connect to public WiFi without VPN active.
- Lock your screen when stepping away (`Win+L` / `Cmd+Ctrl+Q`).
- Do not allow family members to use your company device.
- Enable full-disk encryption — BitLocker (Windows) or FileVault (macOS). IT can verify.
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately to IT (ext. 1001) and HR.

## Core Hours
- All remote employees must be reachable 10am–4pm in their local timezone.
- Video camera on during team meetings unless bandwidth is severely limited.
- Response to messages expected within 30 minutes during core hours.

## Expense Reimbursement
- Internet allowance: ₹1,000/month (submit via expense portal with bill).
- Home office equipment: one-time ₹10,000 allowance with manager approval.
- Submit claims at `https://hrportal.internal/expenses` within 30 days of expense.
